The two you'll open first

A terminal that travels, and the last API client you'll install.

Your shell — split any way you like and reachable from your phone — sitting right next to a full Postman replacement that needs no account and costs nothing.

Terminal

The only terminal you can pick up on your phone — scan a QR and your shell is in your pocket.

A multi-tab, recursively splittable PTY terminal built on xterm.js + a native Rust backend — with a built-in Git panel and a side-by-side diff viewer right beside it. Drag to reorder or split, pin and rename tabs, and keep scrollback across relaunches.

  • One of its kind: scan a QR to drive the same session from your phone over LAN
  • Built-in Git panel + side-by-side diff viewer, no context switch
  • Recursive split panes, pinned tabs & scrollback that survives a restart

API Client

A full Postman replacement, built right in — no subscription, no seat fees, ever.

A Postman-style HTTP client that lives where you code: collections, environments, variables, cookies, response snapshots and full execution history. Free, local, and entirely yours.

  • Collections, environments & variables, organized like Postman
  • Cookies, response snapshots & full execution history
  • No account, no cloud sync, no per-seat pricing
And these alone are worth the install

The features that make devs switch for good.

Mock Server

Start as many servers as you need — each with its own dummy data — in seconds.

Backend not ready? Spin up local mock servers, stub any response, and stop waiting on a teammate's endpoint to test your own work.

Library

A revolutionary app that generates a book chapter-by-chapter — at any length — like nothing else does.

Name a topic, a book, or even a URL and Genisys builds you a progressive, easy-to-understand knowledge base one chapter at a time — with translations, highlights and quizzes. First of its kind.

Messages

Worried your org logs every message to train its AI? Move the conversation here.

Peer-to-peer, end-to-end encrypted LAN chat over the Noise protocol. Nothing touches a server. Nothing is ever written to disk.

QuickShare

The whole world built clouds just to move one file between two machines. You just scan a QR.

Beam files and text to any nearby device over WebRTC — HTTP fallback, optional zip bundling. No cloud, no cable, no account, no upload.

Monitor

Turn this machine into a live camera your phone can watch from anywhere on Wi-Fi.

A phone on the same network scans a QR, gets approved, then watches and listens live over WebRTC — no app to install on the other end.

Why devs trust it

Built the way you'd build it — local-first and yours.

No accounts. No server round-trips for your data. No vendor lock-in on AI.

Your data stays put

Everything lives in local SQLite on your disk. Private LAN messages are never even written to it. Full stop.

Bring your own key

Plug in OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Pay your provider directly — we're never in the middle.

Voice, on-device

Talk to it and it talks back — Whisper speech-to-text and offline TTS run entirely on your machine. No audio uploads.

⌘⇧P to do anything

A global command palette with fuzzy search — ⌘P to jump to any app or item, ⌘⇧P to run any action (Ctrl on Windows & Linux). Themeable down to the tokens.

Native & fast

A Rust + Tauri 2 backend means real native integrations — Git, PTYs, the file system, global shortcuts — at a fraction of an Electron footprint.

Open source, MIT

Read every line, audit the data flow, fork it, or build from source. No black boxes around your tools.
