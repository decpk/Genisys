The two you'll open first

A terminal and an AI chat that actually know your project.

Most apps bolt AI onto the side. Genisys wires it through the middle — so your shell and your assistant share the same files, repos, and tools.

Terminal

A real shell you can split any way you like — then beam a tab to your phone.

Multi-tab, recursively splittable PTY terminal built on xterm.js + a native Rust backend. Drag to reorder or split, pin and rename tabs, and keep your scrollback across relaunches.

  • Recursive split panes, drag-to-reorder & pin
  • Scrollback that survives a restart
  • One-tap share a session to your phone over LAN

Chat

Chat that can actually touch your apps — not just talk about them.

A streaming AI assistant with real tool calls into your files, prompts, API client, notes and more. Full history, per-message context modes, snippet insertion, and inline clickable references.

  • Real tool calls across every Genisys app
  • Bring your own key — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or any endpoint
  • Streaming replies, snippets & clickable entity links

Why devs trust it

Built the way you'd build it — local-first and yours.

No accounts. No server round-trips for your data. No vendor lock-in on AI.

Your data stays put

Everything lives in local SQLite on your disk. Private LAN messages are never even written to it. Full stop.

Bring your own key

Plug in OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Pay your provider directly — we're never in the middle.

Voice, on-device

Talk to it and it talks back — Whisper speech-to-text and offline TTS run entirely on your machine. No audio uploads.

⌘K to do anything

A global command palette with fuzzy search jumps you to any app, action, or item in a keystroke. Themeable down to the tokens.

Native & fast

A Rust + Tauri 2 backend means real native integrations — Git, PTYs, the file system, global shortcuts — at a fraction of an Electron footprint.

Open source, MIT

Read every line, audit the data flow, fork it, or build from source. No black boxes around your tools.
